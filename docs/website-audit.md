# Website audit report

## Summary
The current site already has a usable base, but several parts still feel more like a marketing landing page than a practical mechanical construction company website. The homepage should communicate trust quickly, while the supporting pages should make it easy for a customer to understand the service scope, see proof of delivery, and contact the company directly.

## Page-by-page decisions

### Home page
1. Does this help the customer? Yes.
2. Does this help the company sell? Yes.
3. Can this section be deleted? Yes, if it does not answer one of the five core questions: who we are, what we build, why choose us, what projects we have done, how to contact us.

### Services page
1. Does this help the customer? Yes.
2. Does this help the company sell? Yes.
3. Can this section be deleted? No, but it should be simplified to a direct service catalog with short descriptions and a clear quote CTA.

### Projects page
1. Does this help the customer? Yes.
2. Does this help the company sell? Yes.
3. Can this section be deleted? No, but it should focus on proof of completed work rather than browsing complexity.

### About page
1. Does this help the customer? Yes.
2. Does this help the company sell? Yes.
3. Can this section be deleted? No, but it should be reduced to a short company introduction and a small set of trust points.

### Contact page
1. Does this help the customer? Yes.
2. Does this help the company sell? Yes.
3. Can this section be deleted? No, because direct contact is essential for a construction business.

### Blog pages
1. Does this help the customer? No.
2. Does this help the company sell? No.
3. Can this section be deleted? Yes, especially from the homepage and primary navigation.

## Elements to remove
- FAQ blocks
- Blog preview content
- Fancy counters and decorative metrics
- Glass effects and gradient backgrounds
- Duplicate CTA buttons
- Placeholder text and demo-like content
- Decorative cards that do not support trust

## Design direction
- Keep the experience calm and direct
- Use real construction photos wherever possible
- Favor readable typography and generous spacing
- Use a restrained palette of white, dark blue, steel gray, and orange
- Keep each section focused on a single question
