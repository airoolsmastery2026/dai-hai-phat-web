# DHP Research

Use for external research that supports Đại Hải Phát product, engineering, content, sourcing, or market decisions.

## Method

1. Define the decision the research must support.
2. Prefer primary/official sources for technical, product, price, policy, API, and software facts.
3. Record source date and distinguish current facts from historical information.
4. Cross-check material claims when consequences are meaningful.
5. Separate sourced facts, calculations, and inference.
6. Convert useful findings into reusable structured project knowledge when requested.

## Rules

- Do not copy competitor creative assets or text verbatim.
- Do not treat promotional claims as verified engineering facts.
- Do not invent supplier prices, availability, specifications, or certificates.
- Flag stale pricing/catalog data.
- For software/provider research, verify current official documentation before changing routing or integrations.
- Preserve customer/company confidentiality when using external tools.
