# DHP Estimation

Use for quantity takeoff support, option comparison, indicative cost ranges, and preparing data for formal quotation.

## Inputs

Prefer, in order:

1. Approved drawings/specifications and measured dimensions.
2. Structured DHP price/material/product/supplier data in the repository or connected knowledge base.
3. Current verified supplier/market data when explicitly researched.
4. Clearly stated assumptions only when required data is missing.

## Rules

- Preserve units and show calculation basis.
- Separate quantities, unit rates, labor, accessories, waste factors, transport, tax, and exclusions when applicable.
- Never invent an exact price merely to complete a table.
- Mark estimates as indicative until inputs and current rates are verified.
- Identify missing dimensions/specifications that materially affect cost.
- Do not turn an AI estimate into an approved structural design or binding commercial quotation without the required human/project review.
- Prefer reusable structured output for proposal/CRM/quotation workflows.
