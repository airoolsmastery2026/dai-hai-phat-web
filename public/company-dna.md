# Company DNA

## Mission
Create practical, durable living spaces through verified site data, suitable materials, and reliable residential installation.

## Vision
Become a trusted design-and-build partner for homeowners improving townhouses, apartments, villas, and small commercial spaces.

## Tone
Professional, technical, dependable, and solution-oriented.

## Focus
Residential interiors, gates, fences, stairs, railings, canopies, wall finishes, and custom civil metalwork for living spaces.
