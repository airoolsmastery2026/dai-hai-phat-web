# Taste — anti-generic preflight

Read this phase after foundation and UX decisions are stable, immediately
before implementation. The conceptual source is
[`taste-skill`](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill); use its anti-slop lens
without importing its aesthetic defaults.

## Remove default-looking decisions

Question each proposed element:

- Does this card, pill, gradient, glow, shadow, or animation communicate real
  information or action?
- Is the hierarchy specific to residential design and engineering, or could it
  be pasted into a generic SaaS landing page?
- Is a large hero, repeated CTA, or equal-card grid hiding the next useful step
  on mobile?
- Is copy concrete, truthful Vietnamese from the customer's point of view?
- Can tonal contrast, spacing, or a divider replace glass, blur, or decoration?

Keep DHP decisions that a generic taste guide might normally reject when they
are explicitly canonical. Inter, the ivory/mineral palette, compact rounded
surfaces, and the public AI consultation are intentional project choices.

Record a short Keep / Adapt / Reject list. Implement only the accepted items.
